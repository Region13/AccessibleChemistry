About and review notes

Accessibility features in this tool

  • Every function works by touch, mouse, keyboard, switch or eye gaze. Placement is tap to place, with no dragging.
  • Targets are at least 40 px and mostly 50 px or larger, spaced apart, and nothing scrolls on a tablet in landscape. Long lists are paged.
  • Settings: text size, an optional 24 px minimum text mode, Andika or Atkinson Hyperlegible font, light, dark and high contrast themes, increased text spacing, dwell click with adjustable time, voice output with a Stop button, and an optional calculator.
  • Selected buttons use a bar and a fill, not color alone. Toggles use aria-pressed. Results are announced in a live region.
  • The optional passcode only deters accidental changes. It is stored in this browser, and clearing the browser's site data removes it.
  • No time limits, no flashing and no automatic audio. The page language is set to English.
  • Pasted documents keep subscripts and charges as text. Structures and fraction calculations paste as pictures with alt text.

Standards used

  • Content aligned to the Texas Chemistry TEKS, 19 TAC §112.43: Formula and Name tabs (7)(B) and (12)(A); Structure (6)(E) and (7)(C); Table (5)(B) and (5)(C); Math and Calculator (8), (9)(C), (11)(E), (11)(F), (12)(E) and (13)(D); Atoms (6)(B), (6)(E) and (14)(A); Data (1)(F); Words (3)(B) and (9)(A). Checked against the Cornell LII copy, not the official TEA text.
  • Accessibility design follows WCAG 2.2 AA, CAST UDL Guidelines and CITES guidance.

Source details to verify

  • The polyatomic ion list, electronegativity values and naming conventions are not copied from the class sheets. The teacher should compare them against the Unit 6 and Unit 7 materials.
  • The Unit 7 key spells "tetraoxide" but "octoxide". The tool drops the final vowel only for mono- and octa-.

Needs a person before classroom use

  • Testing with a screen reader.
  • Scanning access (board or auditory) is not included. It can be added for students who need it.
  • Testing with the student's eye tracking or AssistiveTouch on her own device.
  • A review of target size, spacing and dwell time by her AT specialist or OT.
  • A check of the voice output. It uses the device voice and reads subscripts as "sub".
  • A trial with the student and teacher.
  • The scripted checks run so far are not a formal WCAG conformance audit.
